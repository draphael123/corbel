CORBEL
They are running from something, and the far side is the only safe ground. Build the span from timber and rope — then hold it while they cross.

How to play

You are building a bridge across a fixed gap, out of a fixed stock of materials. When you are ready, a procession crosses it. The span never gets longer — the crowd gets heavier.

Drag
Lay a member between two joints. A new member must start from a joint or anchor that already exists — you cannot build in mid-air.
1 · Timber
Takes both push and pull. Priced by length, and the longer you cut it the sooner it buckles.
2 · Rope
Only pulls. It hangs slack until something draws it taut, and it spans much further than timber.
Right-click
Remove a member and refund it.
Space
Send them across. Press again to call it off.
R
Start the crossing over.

Why a flat deck falls

Colour is load. Timber runs from its own brown through amber to red as force passes through it. If a member goes red it is about to fail — brace it, shorten it, or hang some of the weight from rope. Watching where the colour gathers tells you what to build next.
